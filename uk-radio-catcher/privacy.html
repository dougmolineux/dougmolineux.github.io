01

Introduction

Welcome to UK Radio Catcher ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are deeply committed to protecting it. We built UK Radio Catcher with a privacy-first architecture designed to let you enjoy live British radio broadcasts across your Apple devices with minimal data footprint.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you download, install, and use our iOS and Apple CarPlay application. By using UK Radio Catcher, you agree to the minimal, transparent data practices described in this policy.

02

Information We Collect

Our application is engineered to function autonomously without requiring you to surrender your personal identity or private information.

Personal Data:

We explicitly state that we do NOT require user registration, account creation, or login credentials. Furthermore, we do not collect, transmit, or store sensitive personal data of any kind, including:

  • Names, usernames, or physical addresses
  • Email addresses or phone numbers
  • Precise GPS location or real-time spatial tracking
  • Payment information or credit card details

Local Device Data:

To provide a personalized streaming experience, UK Radio Catcher saves your custom app preferences locally on your hardware. This includes:

  • Saved Favorite stations and custom ordering
  • Recently played stations history
  • Sleep timer configurations and playback preferences
  • Radio alarms you set, and a cached copy of the station directory
🔒

Local Storage Note: This data stays on your device. It is not synced to iCloud or sent to us, and we cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

03

Third-Party Radio Streams & Network Connectivity

UK Radio Catcher acts as a dedicated streaming client. To deliver live radio broadcasts to your headphones, speakers, or car audio system, the app connects directly over the internet to third-party streaming servers and broadcast networks (such as BBC, commercial UK broadcasters, and independent radio servers).

When you initiate an audio stream, standard network protocols require your device to establish a direct connection with the broadcaster's server. During this technical exchange, the following standard network telemetry is transmitted directly to the third-party broadcaster:

  • IP Address: Required by streaming servers to route audio data packets back to your device and verify regional broadcast licensing.
  • Device User-Agent: Standard technical identifier (e.g., audio player type and iOS version) used by broadcast servers to optimize audio codec delivery (such as AAC or MP3 streams).

These data transmissions occur directly between your iOS/CarPlay device and the respective radio station provider. We do not proxy, intercept, or record these audio streams. Your interaction with third-party radio broadcasts is subject to the respective privacy policies and terms of service of each individual broadcaster.

Station Directory (Radio Browser):

Station lists, search results and the Station Map come from Radio Browser, a free, community-run directory of radio stations. To show them, the app sends Radio Browser the request itself: for example the words you type into search, a genre, or the area of the map you are looking at. The Station Map shows the area you scroll to; it does not use your location. These requests carry no account or identifier from us, but like any web request they reveal your IP address to Radio Browser's servers. Station logos load directly from the websites that host them.

04

Radio Alarms and In-App Purchases

Radio Alarm Clock: If you set a radio alarm, the app asks for permission to schedule alarms, and iOS (Apple's AlarmKit) wakes you at the time you chose. Your alarms are stored on your device and are not sent to us.

In-App Purchases: The Radio Alarm Clock and Station Map unlocks and the optional "Buy the Developer a Coffee" tip are sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment. We never see your name, Apple Account or card details; the app only learns from Apple which unlocks you own, so it can turn them on and offer Restore Purchases.

05

Analytics and Crash Reporting (Apple Services)

We do not embed third-party tracking SDKs, advertising networks, or cross-app surveillance tools in UK Radio Catcher.

We may receive anonymized, aggregated diagnostic and crash reports provided exclusively through Apple's standard App Store developer tools. This telemetry is only shared with us if you have explicitly opted into sharing diagnostics with app developers in your device's iOS system settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements).

These Apple-provided reports contain stripped, non-identifiable technical data—such as stack traces, memory usage at the time of a crash, iOS OS version, and device model—and are used solely to improve app stability, fix bugs, and optimize playback performance across iOS and Apple CarPlay.

06

Children's Privacy

UK Radio Catcher is a general audience application and is safe for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

Because our app requires zero registration and collects no personal data, no personal information regarding children is ever captured or stored by us. If a parent or guardian believes that a child has somehow provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate remedial action.
