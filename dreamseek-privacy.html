Dreamseek Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

The Short Version

Your dreams never leave your iPhone. Dreamseek has no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no tracking. Recording, transcription, analysis, pictures and videos all happen on your device, and we never see any of it.

Your Dream Journal

Your dreams, titles, notes, analysis and pictures are stored only on your iPhone. They are not synced to iCloud or sent to us or anyone else. You can delete a dream from its page, or every dream in Settings; deleting the app removes everything.

Voice Recording

When you record a dream, Dreamseek asks for permission to use the microphone and speech recognition. Your voice is turned into text by Apple's speech recognition on your iPhone only: Dreamseek requires on-device recognition, so the audio is never sent to Apple's servers. If on-device recognition isn't available, Dreamseek tells you and you can type instead. The audio itself is not saved.

Analysis, Pictures and Dream Reel

  • Dream analysis and chat (themes, mood, people and places, interpretations) use Apple Intelligence's on-device language model.
  • Dream pictures are made on your iPhone with Apple's Image Playground or an on-device image model.
  • Dream Reel videos are put together on your iPhone from on-device scenes and pictures.
  • Dream Narrator reads dreams aloud with the voices built into iOS.

None of this sends your dreams to us or to a cloud service.

Photos and Sharing

If you save a dream picture or a Dream Reel video, Dreamseek asks for permission to add it to your photo library. It only adds items; it doesn't read your existing photos. Sharing happens only when you tap Share and pick where it goes.

Sponsored Amazon Picks

The Lucid Dreaming page shows a few products from Amazon, labelled "Ad". Dreamseek is part of the Amazon Associates program, so if you tap one, Amazon opens with our associate tag and we may earn a commission on what you buy. The app sends Amazon nothing about you or your dreams. Once you're on Amazon, Amazon's privacy notice applies. Buying Remove Ads, or any other Dreamseek unlock, hides these picks.

In-App Purchases

Dream Narrator, Export Dream Photos, Remove Ads, Dream Reel credits and the optional "Buy the Developer a Coffee" tip are sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment. We never see your name, Apple Account or card details; the app only learns from Apple what you own, so it can turn it on and offer Restore Purchases.

What We Don't Do

  • No accounts or sign-ups
  • No third-party analytics, ad networks or tracking across apps and websites
  • No uploading, selling or sharing of your dreams or personal information

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements), Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. They don't include your dreams, and we use them only to fix bugs.

Children

Dreamseek doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and Contact

If Dreamseek changes how it handles information, this page will be updated along with the date at the top.
