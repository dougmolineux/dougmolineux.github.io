Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Introduction

Three Lions is a football management game for iPhone, made by an independent developer. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play. The short version: the game has no accounts, no servers, no ads and no analytics. Everything you do in the game stays on your device.

1. What Stays on Your Device

The game saves these on your device only:

  • Your tournament progress and saved games
  • Match results, goal records and the goal-difference leaderboard
  • Your Tactical Coins balance and settings

None of this is sent to us or anyone else, and it is not synced to iCloud. The leaderboard is your own record of your best runs; it is not shared online. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

2. Coaching with Apple Intelligence

On devices that support Apple Intelligence, the coaching instructions you type or speak are turned into tactics by Apple's on-device language model. That processing happens on your device, and your instructions are not sent to us or to any other server by the game.

3. Voice Dictation

If you use the microphone to speak a coaching instruction, the app asks for permission to use the microphone and speech recognition. Your speech is transcribed by Apple's speech recognition service. Depending on your device and language, Apple may process the audio on its servers to transcribe it; that is covered by Apple's privacy policy. The game itself does not record, store or send your voice anywhere. Dictation is optional: you can always type.

4. In-App Purchases

Tactical Coins and the optional "Buy the Developer a Coffee" tip are sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment. We never see your name, Apple Account or card details; the game only learns from Apple that a purchase went through, so it can add your coins.

5. What We Don't Do

  • No accounts, sign-ups or logins
  • No advertising and no tracking across apps or websites
  • No third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools
  • No selling or sharing of personal information

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements), Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. They don't identify you, and we use them only to fix bugs.

6. Children's Privacy

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Purchases go through Apple, where parents can use Ask to Buy and Screen Time.

7. Changes to This Policy

If the game changes how it handles information, this page will be updated and the date below will change.
